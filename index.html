Mesis experience

Fast to find. Easy to control.

A compact client interface built around quick discovery, clear controls, visual customization, and focused tools without burying everything behind menus.

Visual FX

Custom glint, sky effects, glow controls and a cleaner visual layer that stays configurable.

Finder tools

World and block utilities organized around clear signals, focused results and readable overlays.

Compact GUI

Search-first navigation, direct keybind editing, theme accents and less wasted screen space.

Combat utilities

Quick-access modules and settings designed so you can reach what you need without digging through pages.

Profiles & config

Keep different setups ready for different play styles and swap between them without rebuilding everything.

Community

Discord access is built directly into the client and the site for support, testing and updates.

Quick start

Three steps to launch.

Get the client

Use the current Mesis build provided through the project or community release channel.

Place it in mods

Use the matching Fabric / Minecraft version and place the Mesis JAR in your mods folder.

Open Mesis

Launch Minecraft, open the GUI, search a module and change its keybind or settings directly.
